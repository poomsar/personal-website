## Interests

Wind turbine blades, structural health monitoring, energy storage, and sensor networks. Mostly, I'm interested in structures that can sense, store energy, or otherwise do more than carry load.

## Expertise

Wind energy, battery energy storage, and the hybrid systems that combine them. My core work is technical risk assessment and FMEA, technical due diligence, and technical and commercial feasibility, with compliance to industry standards like DNVGL and IEC built in. On the people side, I bring systems engineering, project management, and leading multidisciplinary teams from early concept to delivery.

## Experience

Blade structural engineer at Siemens Gamesa since 2019, now a senior, working remotely from Bangkok with the team in Denmark. Before that: research and technology advising at Stanford, running the IWSHM conference and the structures lab, plus a couple of short maintenance internships in Thailand.
